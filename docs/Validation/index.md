# Validation

This tab lists the tests that compare Aragog with an independent result: a published benchmark, an analytical limit or a second implementation. Each case in the table carries the `reference_pinned` marker. Where a page section exists, the table links it.

- [Core](core.md): the core module against Nimmo (2015)[^cite-nimmo2015a][^cite-nimmo2015b], the `leeds` and `leeds_thermal` models of thermal_history (Greenwood et al. (2021)[^cite-greenwood2021]) and analytical limits.
- [Core alloy liquidus](core_alloy_liquidus.md): the liquidus of iron with S, C and O. At high pressure it is checked against the liquidus curves and eutectic points of Mori et al. (2017[^cite-mori2017]), Mashino et al. (2019[^cite-mashino2019]) and Oka et al. (2019[^cite-oka2019]). From 1 bar to 25 GPa the eutectic is checked against Fei et al. (1997[^cite-fei1997]) and 12 eutectic check points (measured points, fit values and bounds), and the liquidus against Buono & Walker (2011[^cite-buono2011]) and Chen et al. (2008[^cite-chen2008]). The page also checks the rule for several light elements, the slope of the liquidus against pressure and the 2 reads of every curve taken from a figure.
- [Surface boundary](surface.md): the prescribed surface temperature (`outer_boundary_condition = 5`) against closed-form conduction solutions and a grid-convergence study.
- [Surface lid](surface_lid.md): the sub-grid surface lid (`subgrid_lid`) against the freezing of a lava layer, a cooling half-space, a steady crust and a fine mesh, for a solid shell and a shell that freezes from the mush, the mesh and floor dependence of the lid base at the melt temperature, and the lid surface and onset weight under an atmosphere.
- [Phase transitions](phase_transitions.md): the blend of the material properties at the solidus and the liquidus against the blend formula of SPIDER, and the NumPy and JAX right-hand sides inside the blend.
- [Mesh refinement](mesh_refinement.md): the mesh refined towards the surface and the CMB against its set end cells, and the mesh without refinement against the stored results of the code without the option.
- [Numerics](numerics.md): the first-principles checks of the solver, the phase-boundary rate controller, the equation of state and the NumPy and JAX right-hand sides, including the `reference_pinned` solid-rheology solver-wiring benchmarks.
- [Solid-state convection](solid_state_convection.md): the saturating integral activation enthalpy, the viscous mixing-length top-slope benchmarks, the 0-D stagnant-lid comparison and the internal-heating scaling exponents. The Arrhenius creep, plastic yield and stagnant-lid closure have worked numbers on the [Explanation page](../Explanations/solid_state_convection.md). The `reference_pinned` tests of the Arrhenius creep and the yield stress are in the table below; the stagnant-lid closure has unit tests only.

The command of a case is `pytest <test>`; the young-layer case, the surface-boundary cases, the surface-lid cases, the phase-transition case, the solid-rheology solver-wiring cases in [Numerics](numerics.md), and the saturating-activation, top-slope, 0-D stagnant-lid comparison, and internal-heating scaling cases in [Solid-state convection](solid_state_convection.md) each have a subsection with their reference, tolerance, test id and command, and the other cases are described by the prose of their linked section.

`pytest -m reference_pinned` runs every case. The core-page tests need the SPIDER P-S tables (`ARAGOG_TEST_EOS_DIR`, [Build tests](../How-to/build_tests.md)); the stable-layer and Earth-budget cases take minutes.

**Checks on stored runs.** These tests read stored runs and do not run the code. They are not in the table and carry no `reference_pinned` marker. `tests/test_surface_lid_v8.py` compares the lid after its hand-over with 2 runs without the lid: the step between 200 and 400 nodes is at most 0.75 of the step between 100 and 200 nodes, and the late mean of the surface flux lies within 1 % of the reference on 200 and 400 nodes ([Surface lid](surface_lid.md#a-case-where-the-lid-hands-over)).

`tests/test_surface_lid_v5_rows.py::test_stored_rows_keep_their_values_with_the_lid` compares all 36 benchmark rows with and without the lid ([Surface lid](surface_lid.md#the-lid-on-the-stagnant-lid-benchmarks)).

`tests/test_surface_lid_base_cap.py::test_lid_base_cap_on_the_mesh_and_the_floor` is a sensitivity study with no reference: it reads stored runs of the lid base at the melt temperature on 100, 200 and 400 nodes and with floors of 1e2, 1e3 and 1e4 J/kg.

At 3e5 yr the step from 200 to 400 nodes stays below 1.2 (1.024), at 4e5 yr both mesh steps lie above 1.4, and the floors agree within 4e-2 up to 3e5 yr ([Surface lid](surface_lid.md#the-lid-base-at-the-melt-temperature-mesh-and-floor)). `pytest tests/test_surface_lid_base_cap.py tests/test_surface_lid_v5_rows.py tests/test_surface_lid_v8.py -m "not slow" --validation-plots` writes their plot pairs.

## Plots written by the tests

A case with a plot pair shows the tested values against their reference in a light and a dark version. The test writes the pair with the same numbers it asserts, through the `validation_plot` fixture of proteus-mpl. This command writes the pairs of the table to `docs/assets/validation/`; the pairs of the checks on stored runs come from the command above:

```console
pytest -m reference_pinned --validation-plots
```

Without `--validation-plots` the tests assert and write nothing. The figures in `docs/figures/vv/` are made by the scripts in `tools/verification/`, not by the tests.
