# `aragog.core`

The `aragog.core` package carries the core evolution module: the core as a component with its own state rather than an isothermal reservoir. The radial structure comes from 1 of 2 sources. The closed-form Gaussian profile family of Labrosse et al. (2001)[^cite-labrosse2001] and Nimmo (2015)[^cite-nimmo2015b] makes every budget term an analytic integral or a fixed-order quadrature, so the module stays at ODE cost. A Zalmoxis core on a PALEOS liquid iron isentrope is tabulated once per structure. The capacities, the adiabat and the rates run through `jax.numpy` at 64-bit precision and are jit- and grad-safe. The heat content and the heat change of a call, and on a tabulated profile also `secular_heat_change`, `t_cmb_at_centre` and `outside_calibration_fraction`, run eagerly on concrete values.

| Name | Role |
|------|------|
| `GaussianCoreProfiles` | Density, enclosed mass, gravity (exact erf form), hydrostatic pressure (quadrature or the printed Labrosse closed form), gravitational potential, and the adiabat of the core. |
| `LiquidIronTable` | The liquid side of the PALEOS iron EOS that Zalmoxis writes (a `.dat` table or its `.npz` cut), refused unless its `# Kind:` line names `liquid`; bicubic interpolation in $\log_{10} P$ and $\log_{10} T$ of $\rho$, $u$, $s$, $c_p$, $c_v$, $\alpha$ and $\nabla_\mathrm{ad}$ (SI units), with the entropy derivatives `ds_dT` and `ds_dP`. |
| `TabulatedCoreProfiles`, `load_core_arrays` | A Zalmoxis core (`r` \[m\], `m` \[kg\], `P` \[Pa\], `rho` \[kg m$^{-3}$\], `g` \[m s$^{-2}$\] from an `.npz`) on one isentrope of the liquid table: the same interface as the Gaussian profile, plus a secular heat capacity and heat change that depend on $T_\mathrm{cmb}$ and the mass fraction outside the calibration window of the liquid ([explanation](../../Explanations/core_tabulated.md)). |
| `IronMeltingCurve` | Pure-iron melting curve (Anzellini et al. (2013)[^cite-anzellini2013], the PALEOS prescription) with a multiplicative light-element depression. |
| `AlloyLiquidus`, `eutectic` | Liquidus of iron with S, C and O (Si, H and N lower no melting point): ideal-solution binaries on Fe$_k$X$_m$ units through their eutectic points, joined by a harmonic mean of their $A$; `t_melt` \[K\] and `gradient` \[K/Pa\] of pressure \[Pa\], `status` flags for extrapolated pressures and compositions past the eutectic ([explanation](../../Explanations/core_alloy_liquidus.md)). |
| `QuadraticMeltingCurve` | The quadratic-in-pressure parameterisation of Nimmo (2015), ch. 9.08, Eq. 6, interchangeable with the iron curve; the form benchmark models are defined in. |
| `CoreEnergyBudget` | Effective-heat-capacity energy balance: secular cooling, inner-core-nucleation latent heat, and the gravitational energy of light-element rejection; a `legacy` mode reproduces the isothermal-reservoir closure exactly. |
| `CoreEntropyBudget` | Entropy balance on top of the energy budget: cooling, latent, gravitational, and radiogenic sources against the conduction sink (closed form for the Gaussian adiabat), the dynamo entropy margin, and field strength via the energy-flux scaling of Christensen et al. (2009)[^cite-christensen2009]. |
| `CoreModule` | Standalone coupling: holds the core state and advances it over an externally supplied heat-flow interval with jit-compiled Runge-Kutta sub-steps, recording the sub-step trajectory. |
| `build_core_module_budget` | Config-dict factory for the solver coupling (`core_bc = 'core_module'`); geometry always comes from the mesh. |
| `check_ra_crit` | Validate the CMB critical Rayleigh number: None (Thiriet et al. (2019)[^cite-thiriet2019] eq. 16), `'phase'` (the law by phase), or a positive, finite real number, returned as a float. |
| `liquid_weight`, `check_liquid_band`, `RA_CRIT_PHASE` | The law by phase: the weight $(1 + \tanh((\phi_0 - \phi_\text{rheo})/w_s))/2$ of the liquid law ($\mathrm{Ra}_\text{crit} = 450$) at the melt fraction $\phi_0$ of the bottom cell, with $w_s = (w/2)/\operatorname{artanh} 0.9$, so 0.05 and 0.95 at the band edges, with the switch at the critical melt fraction of Miyazaki & Korenaga (2019b)[^cite-miyazaki2019b]; the check refuses a band $[\phi_\text{rheo} - w/2, \phi_\text{rheo} + w/2]$ outside \[0, 1\] ([explanation](../../Explanations/core_bc.md#critical-rayleigh-number-by-phase)). |
| `critical_rayleigh_eq16`, `RA_CRIT_UPPER_LAYER` | The lower-layer critical Rayleigh number $0.28\,Ra_i^{0.21}$ of Thiriet et al. (2019)[^cite-thiriet2019] eq. 16, from the internal Rayleigh number of the mantle; the fixed upper-layer value 450 of their Table 2. |
| `cmb_boundary_layer_flux` | CMB heat flux of the solver coupling from the core-mantle temperature contrast through a mantle-side boundary layer (Foley & Driscoll (2016)[^cite-foley2016]; Thiriet et al. (2019)[^cite-thiriet2019]) for a hot core under a buoyant layer, else conduction across the bottom half cell; with `liquid_band` it blends $\ln \mathrm{Ra}_\text{crit}$ of 450 and eq. 16 by `liquid_weight`. |
| `crystallization_regime` | Regime code from the superheat profile: fully liquid, bottom-up, top-down, snow, or fully frozen (taxonomy of Breuer et al. (2015)[^cite-breuer2015]). `refuse_unmodelled_regime` raises for the regimes the budget does not model. |
| `adiabatic_ratio`, `stratification_depth` | Subadiabatic-onset ratio and the equilibrium thickness of the stably stratified sub-CMB layer from conductive matching. |

The budget terms are cross-validated against the open-source Leeds `thermal_history` implementation (secular exact, boundary terms to 0.5%), and the profile and melting machinery is pinned against Nimmo (2015), ch. 9.08, Table 2 (adiabatic length scales, ICB temperatures, melting gradients, adiabatic heat flows). The budget assumes bottom-up crystallization; parameter sets whose melting curve rises above the adiabat at the CMB (a top-down or snow topology) book no boundary terms, and the solver refuses a call that enters such a regime.
